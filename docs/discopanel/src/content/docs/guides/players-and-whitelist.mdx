DiscoPanel tracks every player who connects through its proxy, keeps their play history, and manages whitelists and bans from the panel — including syncing them to servers over RCON.

## How tracking works

The built-in proxy inspects Minecraft connections as they pass through and observes each client's **Login Start** packet — the moment the player's username becomes known. From there DiscoPanel records a session: who joined, which server they were routed to, when they joined and left, and how much data was transferred in each direction. Sessions survive panel restarts; anything left open by a crash is closed up on the next start.

<Aside type="note">
Only traffic routed through the DiscoPanel proxy is tracked. Players who connect to a server's port directly bypass the proxy and won't appear in the Players page, session history, or traffic summaries.
</Aside>

Tracking is per server, so on a network with a lobby and several backends you get per-server playtime breakdowns for each player, not just a total.

## The Players page

The **Players** page in the sidebar has three parts:

- **Online Now** — players currently connected through the proxy, with the server they're on and when they joined. The dashboard home shows the same card, so you can see who's on at a glance.
- **Player list** — every player DiscoPanel has seen, with first/last seen, session count, and total playtime. Search by name and filter by server from the dropdown.
- **Player detail** — click a player for their per-server stats (playtime, sessions, last seen) and their most recent sessions, including duration and bytes in/out.

Join and leave moments raise **Player join** and **Player leave** events, which you can trigger on with event-based [webhook tasks](/guides/metrics-and-alerts/#delivering-alerts-via-webhooks) or module hooks — announce joins in Discord, or start a stats module when the first player connects.

## Panel-side whitelist

The **Whitelist & Bans** section of the Players page holds the panel-side whitelist: your desired list of who may play. Entries are stored in DiscoPanel's database with an optional note (useful for tracking who someone is or when they were added).

The whitelist in the panel is the source of truth; servers are converged to it over RCON:

- **Apply to servers** — select one or more running servers and DiscoPanel diffs each server's current in-game whitelist against the panel list, then adds and removes only the difference. Per-server results show exactly what was added and removed.
- **Pull from server** — read a server's current whitelist via RCON and import it into the panel. Handy when you've been running `whitelist add` in-game and want the panel to catch up.

<Aside type="caution">
Applying and pulling require the server to be running, since both go through RCON. Make sure RCON is enabled on the servers you want to manage this way.
</Aside>

You can also toggle the whitelist on or off while a server is running — see the [Branding tab](#the-branding-tab) below.

## Bans across servers

From the same section, **ban** or **unban** a player on any selection of servers with one action. DiscoPanel runs the ban or pardon commands via RCON and reports the result per server, so a cheater caught on one backend can be removed from the whole network in a couple of clicks.

Bans are applied directly to the selected servers — they don't live in the panel-side whitelist, so they don't touch your allow-list.

## The Branding tab

Each server has a **Branding** tab that edits how the server presents itself, in one place:

- **MOTD** — the message of the day shown in the server list.
- **Server icon** — the `server-icon.png` players see in their client (64 KB PNG max).
- **Max players** and **online mode** — written to `server.properties`.
- **Whitelist toggle** — flips `white-list` in `server.properties` **live**: when the server is running, DiscoPanel writes the setting and issues the RCON reload, so you can open or close the server without a restart.

<Aside type="note">
The whitelist toggle and MOTD apply immediately on a running server. Changing **online mode** or **max players** rewrites `server.properties` but needs a restart to take effect.
</Aside>
